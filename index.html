THE LETTER I WANTED TO SAY PROPERLY

For my Chaklii 🐦

SINCE 03 MAY 2024

Dear Chaklii ❤️

There are things I can say easily when I am joking, and there are things I feel so deeply that I never seem to find the perfect words for them. I made this little movie because an ordinary message felt too small for everything I wanted you to know.

When I think about us, I do not only remember the big moments. I remember the small ones: a random message that changed my mood, a stupid joke that made us laugh, a conversation that went on longer than planned, and those ordinary minutes that somehow became the part of the day I looked forward to most.

You have become important to me in hundreds of quiet ways.

I notice your effort. I notice when you care even when you do not make a big show of it. I notice the little things you remember. I notice the times you try to understand me, even when I make that difficult.

And I know I have not always returned that care perfectly. Sometimes I tease when I should listen. Sometimes I defend myself when I should simply understand. Sometimes I realise too late that a small thing hurt you more than I thought it would.

For those moments, I do not want to give you a dramatic promise that I will suddenly become perfect. I would rather give you something more real: I will keep learning. I will keep listening. I will keep trying to become better at loving someone whose feelings genuinely matter to me.

Because you genuinely matter to me.

Not only when we are laughing. Not only when everything is easy. You matter when we misunderstand each other, when one of us is stubborn, when the mood is bad, and when saying “I am sorry” takes more courage than it should.

I do not want us to be a perfect story. I want us to be an honest one—two people who can be silly, dramatic, annoying, caring, imperfect, and still choose to treat each other with more patience after every lesson.

Since 03 May 2024, time has been collecting tiny pieces of us. Some are funny. Some are messy. Some are soft. Some are probably too ridiculous to explain to anyone else. But they are ours, and I am grateful for them.

If you remember only one thing from this entire movie, remember this: your place in my life is not measured by one perfect day.

It is in the consistency of missing your messages when they are not there. It is in wanting to tell you something the moment it happens. It is in caring whether you are okay. It is in the fact that, after the jokes and arguments and normal everyday chaos, you are still one of the first people my mind looks for.

I hope I keep giving you reasons to feel valued, respected, heard, and safe being exactly who you are. I hope I never become too comfortable to keep making effort. I hope we keep finding our way back to laughter without ignoring the things we need to learn from.

You are one of the most meaningful parts of my ordinary days, Chaklii. And ordinary days are most of life. That is why this means so much to me. ❤️

Thank you for the care you give. Thank you for the memories we already have. Thank you for every silly conversation that somehow became important just because it was with you.

Whatever the next chapter looks like, I want to meet it with more patience, more honesty, more effort, and the same ridiculous Popat humour you already know too well. 😌

This whole movie was made for one reason: I wanted you to see, for a few minutes, how much space you quietly occupy in my heart and in my everyday life.

Yours,
Popat 🦜❤️
